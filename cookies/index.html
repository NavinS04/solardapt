Cookies

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This website does not set any cookies, and we don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. That's why there's no cookie banner.

Third parties

  • Fonts: our fonts load from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google's servers to fetch them, which shares your IP address with Google. Google Fonts doesn't set cookies.
  • Booking a call: the booking button takes you to Cal.com, which has its own cookie and privacy notice.

If we ever add analytics or similar tools, we'll update this page and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

Questions? See our privacy notice.
