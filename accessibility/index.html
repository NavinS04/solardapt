Accessibility

Last updated: 26 October 2026

We want everyone to be able to use this site. It's built to work with keyboards and screen readers, respects reduced-motion settings, works at small screen sizes and up to 200% zoom, and uses text colours chosen for contrast.

We haven't yet had it independently audited against WCAG 2.2. If something doesn't work for you, email us or call us, and we'll fix it or get you the information another way.
