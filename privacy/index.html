  • Homeowner enquiry data: as the installer instructs in our data processing agreement, then deleted or returned.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to how we use it, or ask us to restrict it. You can object to direct marketing at any time, and we will stop immediately. Reply "stop" to any message or email us. We'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
