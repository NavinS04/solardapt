The problem

Most installer marketing doesn't fail at the click.
It fails in the hour after.

You pay for the enquiry. Then it lands while your team is on a roof, sits in an inbox, and the homeowner books whoever rang first.

Shared leads

Aggregator leads sold to three or more installers. You're in a race to the bottom on price before you've even spoken to them.

"Free solar" tyre-kickers

Clickbait ads fill the diary with renters and people expecting free panels. Your surveyors drive to homes that were never going to buy.

The missed-call graveyard

Enquiries arrive at 2pm and 8pm, when nobody's at a desk. By the time someone calls back, the homeowner has booked elsewhere.

Booked, then no-show

A survey in the diary isn't a survey that happens. Without reminders and a rebooking route, the slot and the drive are wasted.

Firms that contacted online leads within an hour were nearly seven times as likely to qualify them as firms that waited longer. Harvard Business Review, "The Short Life of Online Sales Leads", 2011.

FAQ

The questions installers ask

Do you sell the same enquiries to other installers?

No. We work with one installer per postcode district, and that's written into the agreement. Your ads run in your own account; the enquiries are yours alone.

Do you guarantee a number of surveys or installs?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. Results depend on your area, offer, pricing and how your team sells. We commit to the work: the system live, every enquiry handled the same way every time, and a weekly report so you can judge for yourself.

Who pays for the ads, and how much?

You do, directly to Meta, in an ad account you own. We agree a daily budget with you in writing before anything goes live and never change it without your written approval. Our fee is separate: a fixed monthly fee plus a one-off setup, explained on the planning call.

How do you keep out "free solar" tyre-kickers?

By not attracting them. The ads don't promise free panels, and the funnel asks about homeownership, property type, roof, bill and timeframe before anyone can book. Renters and people just browsing are thanked and filtered out before they reach your diary.

Do I have to change my CRM or phone system?

No. Alerts come to your phone and email, and bookings go into the calendar you already use or a dedicated survey calendar. If you have a CRM, we'll pass enquiries into it where it supports that.

How fast is "within a minute"?

The text and email are automated and designed to go out within about a minute of the form being submitted, and you get your alert at the same time. They're messages about the homeowner's own enquiry, sent with their permission, and they stop the moment the homeowner replies, books or opts out.

What about GDPR?

For your customers' data, you're the controller and we act as your processor under a written data processing agreement. Homeowners give their permission when they enquire, every message carries an opt-out, and opt-outs are honoured immediately.

What's the contract?

Rolling monthly with 30 days' notice. If we don't deliver the agreed service milestones (system live, workflows running, weekly reports sent), you can pause the monthly fee until we do.

Why now?

Homeowners can install solar at 0% VAT until 31 March 2027. The installers with a working enquiry system in place before then are the ones who'll handle that demand properly.

Ready to stop paying for enquiries you don't follow up?

A 20-minute planning call. We'll look at your current enquiry flow and numbers, and I'll tell you straight whether this is a fit.
